# The app's Options sheet: each setting's label and hint, in the order both apps show them (tools/gen-shared-ui.py
# writes UiText in TouchGuide.kt and TouchGuide.swift).  "id | label | hint"; an empty field is left out.
# "[android] id | …" or "[ios] id | …" overrides the line for one app.

buttonScale | Buttons and menus |
messageScale | Messages |
colours | Colours | Bright lifts the game's dark blues and reds so they read on black; Original is the game's own palette.
itemScale | Inventory and items |
statusScale | Status |
messageLines | Messages shown |
panelTabs | Panel tabs | Icons fit the panel without scrolling; names are the scrolling strip.
tabsOnTop | Panel tabs along the top | The seven tabs always across the top of the screen, each opening its screen at once; tap the open tab to close it.  The panel then fills a portrait screen below them (in landscape, part of the width), and its own tab row, ✕ and the panel button go.  Experimental.
tiles | Tiles on the map | MicroChasm's pictures instead of letters. Lists and menus keep the letters.
dpad | D-pad | Tap a key to step; hold it, or drag from the centre key towards it, to run.  Centre: tap to wait, hold to rest; on a floating pad hold the centre then drag to move it.  Beside status sits under the status in portrait and in the side column in landscape.
dpadSize | D-pad size |
swipe | Swipe on the map | Pan drags the map with one finger, as two fingers always do; Step takes one step that way; Run runs. Aiming and "Which way?" take a swipe in every mode.
autoMore | Skip -more- prompts | Messages are not held for a tap to continue; the message log keeps all of them. This is the game's own "Automatically clear -more- prompts" option, saved with the character.
autoMoreLocked | | Change it from the map, or in Game options → User interface.
itemDetails | Item details in the pack | Says what an ego item gives in general, and what each ability an item grants does, when you open it in the pack.
traceUsage | Record usage trace | Keeps a log on this device of which buttons, panels and tabs you use, never what you type or the game's text, to help improve the controls. Nothing leaves the phone unless you choose Options → Send logs.
tapBuzz | Buzz on taps | A light tick under every touch, like the keyboard.
tapStrength | Tap buzz strength | Stronger or weaker. The slider buzzes when you let go.
fightStrength | Combat buzz strength | Scales every combat and big-moment buzz. The slider gives a sample when you let go.
combatBuzz | Buzz in combat | A faint tick when you swing and miss, a firmer one when your blow lands, a double pulse when it kills, and a stronger buzz whenever you are hurt, the more so the more it costs you.
lowHealthBuzz | Buzz on big moments | When health falls below the game's hitpoint warning (Game options → Hitpoint warning), when you gain a level, are poisoned or paralysed, something is summoned, and when you die.
edgeSwipe | Swipe from the right edge opens the panel | A short stretch in the middle of the right edge, marked by a faint handle, opens the character panel. That stretch no longer works as the system Back gesture.
scumming | Save scumming (for testing) | Adds Scum (keep a copy of your save) and Restore scum save to the ☰ menu, and Restore to the death screen. Restore also lists the last three autosaves, at least five minutes apart.
saves | Saves | Export sends a copy of your save to another app (Drive, mail…). Import loads one back, replacing the current game.
[ios] saves | Saves | Export sends a copy of your save to another app (Files, mail…). Import loads one back, replacing the current game; NarSil then closes, and loads it when opened again.
problems | Problems | Send logs shares a zip of the app's log, your save and character dump, for a bug report.
[ios] problems | Problems | Send logs shares the app's log, your save and character dump, for a bug report.
displayLink | Display and controls | Messages, panel tabs, tiles, d-pad, swipes
